How the calculation works

Net profit = sale price + shipping charged to the buyer − marketplace fees − item cost − your shipping cost − packaging and other costs.

Margin = net profit ÷ revenue. ROI = net profit ÷ everything you spent on the item. Break-even price is the lowest sale price at which your net profit is zero with the numbers above.

About the default fees

eBay (US): a percentage of the total sale (item plus shipping) plus a small per-order fee. Reported standard rates for most categories vary between sources, so check eBay's current fee page and edit the percentage above. Poshmark (US): a flat fee on sales under $15 and a percentage at $15 and above; the buyer pays shipping separately. Mercari (US): a percentage of the item price plus buyer-paid shipping.
