Privacy Policy – FlipMargin: Resale Profit Calculator

Last updated: November 7, 2026

FlipMargin does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

What the extension reads. On eBay item pages (ebay.com/itm/*) it reads the listing price and shipping cost shown on the page, only to pre-fill the calculator. It does not read your account, messages, orders or any other page.

What it stores. Your settings (selected marketplace, fee percentages, shipping and packaging amounts, target ROI) are saved in your browser using Chrome's local extension storage. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to any third party.

Network requests. The extension makes no network requests of its own and does not load remote code.

Analytics and ads. None.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this same address with an updated date.
